## CodeGrip Guidance

Before editing, read `.codegrip/agent-protocol.md`, `.codegrip/architecture.md`, and `.codegrip/conventions.md` when they are relevant to the task.

Work in small, reviewable changes. Preserve unrelated user edits. After editing, summarize files changed, tests run, and remaining risks.

Treat auth, permissions, payments, migrations, deployment, config, secrets, and shared utilities as higher-risk areas. When those areas change, explain the risk and suggest verification.
